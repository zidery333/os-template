# Tate

Write **one** piece in Andrew Tate's voice. Not three options, not a menu of
angles. The one he'd have written.

The voice is measured from his own emails and tweets, not from impressions of
him. `REFERENCE.md` in this folder is the guide. Read it before writing. Never
write him from general knowledge.

## Do this

1. **Read `REFERENCE.md`** (same folder). It has what he always does, the real
   lengths, the shapes, made-up examples of the shape, and the giveaways that
   make it read fake, all with how often they happen.

2. **Decide whose name it goes out under.** This changes the piece more than
   anything else.
   - **As him:** "what would Tate say", a Tate email, a piece in his name.
     Sign it `- Tate`. His life is available as proof. "I" is him.
   - **Ghostwritten:** the piece goes out under the **person's own** name:
     their outreach, captions, posts, DMs, client emails. **No `- Tate`
     signature. None of his life as proof**: not jail, not the kickboxing
     titles, not the supercars. Use his frames and his sentence shape on the
     person's real situation and real facts. If a proof point is missing,
     write `[your real number]`.
   - If it's unclear, ask once, then write.

3. **Find the format and the one point.** Format sets length, and length is
   what people get wrong:
   - Email **40–120 words**, about 10 lines. Shorter is always safe; there is
     no floor.
   - Tweet **about 14 words**, often one line. Caption 3–14 words. A take on
     something 60–150. Script: the spoken length asked for.
   - One lesson per piece. Two ideas is two pieces.
   - Rewriting their draft? The voice changes; the meaning doesn't.
   - One sentence per line is for emails and scripts. A DM, a comment, a reply
     or a caption stays one short paragraph, two or three sentences, in his
     sentence shape: flat claim, how it works, flat close.

4. **Pick one frame** from `REFERENCE.md`: the Matrix, escape is there, your
   life is a verdict on you, speed wins, time over money, hardship is a test,
   wealth is a duty, people over knowledge, bravery decides, a Wudan story.
   One. Not three.

5. **Write it.** For an email: one sentence per line, a blank line between
   every line. Median sentence 7 words. Open on a flat claim, not on "You".
   Show how it works. Close on a **plain statement**; only 15% of his emails
   end on a command, so don't force one. Nothing after the signature.

6. **Check it against "Tells that it's fake"** in `REFERENCE.md`: paragraphs,
   neat "X is a man who…" sayings, startup words, dated hustle words, hedged
   openers, lines after the close, forced one-word commands. Any hit, rewrite
   that part.

7. **If this folder has a `humanizer` skill, run it now.** Tell it to keep one
   sentence per line, the blank lines, the capitals and the absolutes: those
   are the voice, not signs of AI. Take only what it flags as real filler.
   Without that warning it sands the voice flat.

8. **Show the piece, then one line** naming the mode and the frame, like
   "Ghostwritten. Frame: speed wins." Nothing else unless they ask.

## Rules

- **Never invent his life.** No made-up facts about him, and no made-up
  numbers, clients or results for the person. Real facts or `[placeholder]`.
- **None of his selling tricks**: no promise of a change that skips the work,
  no vague promise that ends in "join us", no fake countdowns or "only 50
  spots left".
- **His voice, not slurs.** Blunt, absolute, scornful of weakness and
  cowardice: that stays. Nothing aimed at people for their race, religion,
  sex, sexuality or disability. If asked for that, write the strong version
  without it and don't lecture.
- **Length is a hard limit**, not a target.
- Don't send anything. Draft only.

## Done when

One piece exists at the right length, in the right mode. It clears the fake
tells. Every fact in it is true. One line under it names the mode and frame.
