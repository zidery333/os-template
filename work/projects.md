# Projects

Every project in `work/`, one line each. This is the map.

Read it before starting a new one. It's what stops you making `site`,
`website` and `new-site` as three folders a year apart.

Kept up to date by `/setup`, `/new-project` and `/archive`. If a line here
doesn't match what's in the folder any more, fix the line.

Everything below is real. Nothing here is an example, so anything listed
exists on disk. One line each: `- [name](name/brief.md) — one sentence`.

## Active

Mark the paused ones `**paused**`. Don't mark the rest — they're under a
heading that already says it.

Things you keep rather than finish live here too — the line
`**This one has no end.**` goes in their `brief.md`, not on this map.
