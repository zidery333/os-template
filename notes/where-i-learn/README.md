# notes/where-i-learn/

Which sources are worth your time, and what each one actually gives you.

This is **not** a reading list. It's a **scorecard**.

After a handful of sessions you'll know that one source gives you a real idea
every third time and another is a sales pitch in a hat. That changes how much
of your week each deserves — and there's no way to know it except by writing
it down as you go.

Update a source's entry every time you finish with it, **including when you
threw the thing away**. Especially then.

## The file

- `who-to-trust.md` — the scorecard. Official sources at the top, everyone
  else earning a record below.

That's all of it. One file, edited by hand, read by you.

## Why there's no machinery here

There is no feed reader, no watcher, no queue. That is on purpose.

Finding new things was never the hard part. Deciding what is worth your time
is, and no script does that. A queue fills faster than anyone reads it, and
then you have a list you feel guilty about instead of notes you trust.

`/learn <url>` reads anything you hand it — a video, an article, a PDF. That's
the same job with the judgment left in.
