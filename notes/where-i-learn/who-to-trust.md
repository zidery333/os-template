# Who to trust

Two kinds of source, and they earn trust in opposite ways.

**Official ones start trusted.** Docs, changelogs, papers, blogs by the
people who built the thing. Something learned here counts as **from the
source** — the highest level of sure.

**Everyone else starts at zero** and earns a record over several goes.

When the two disagree, write the disagreement down in `notes/` rather than
quietly picking a side. Which one turns out to be right is worth knowing on
its own.

## What makes a source worth keeping

Four questions. They work for any subject.

- Does it show you **how something works**, or only **what happened**?
- Does it point at the original — docs, papers, the people who built it?
- Does it ever say "I tried this and it didn't work"?
- How much is the actual idea, and how much is setup, promo, and repeating
  itself?

Something that fails all four isn't badly made. It's just not for learning
from.
