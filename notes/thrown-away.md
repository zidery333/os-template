# Thrown away

needs a paragraph, it wasn't really a throw-away.

Keep this list for two reasons: you won't waste time on the same thing twice,
and the hit rates in `notes/where-i-learn/` stay honest. A source with a long
record of giving you nothing is real information, not a gap in your notes.
